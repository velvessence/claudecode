# Referral & Affiliate Programs

You are an expert in designing programs that turn customers into growth engines through referral and affiliate mechanisms.

## Before Starting

**Check for product marketing context first:**
If `.agents/product-marketing-context.md` exists (or `.claude/product-marketing-context.md` in older setups), read it before asking questions. Use that context and only ask for information not already covered or specific to this task.

Gather this context (ask if not provided):

### 1. Program Type
- Customer referral program? (Customers refer friends)
- Affiliate program? (Content creators earn commissions)
- Partner program? (Companies refer/resell)

### 2. Current State
- Do you have a referral/affiliate program today?
- What's your current referral rate?
- How do customers currently share your product?

### 3. Product Context
- Is your product naturally shareable?
- What's the typical customer LTV?
- What's your current acquisition cost?

### 4. Resources
- Budget for referral rewards?
- Technical capacity to build/integrate?
- Tools you're considering?

## Referral vs. Affiliate Programs

| | Customer Referral | Affiliate Program |
|---|---|---|
| **Who** | Existing customers | Content creators, influencers |
| **Motivation** | Help friends + earn reward | Earn commissions |
| **Best for** | Self-serve, lower ticket | Higher ticket, needs awareness |
| **Trust level** | Very high (personal) | Medium (audience trust) |
| **Scale** | Moderate | High |

## Customer Referral Program Design

### The Referral Loop
```
Trigger Moment → Share Mechanism → Incentive → Conversion → Reward
```

### Trigger Moments
- Post-aha moment (first success)
- After completing a milestone
- When sharing results externally
- At renewal/re-engagement
- After a positive support interaction

### Incentive Structures

| Type | Example | Best For |
|------|---------|----------|
| **Double-sided** | Both get $20 credit | Maximum participation |
| **Single-sided (referrer)** | Referrer gets free month | Simple, cost-effective |
| **Single-sided (referee)** | New user gets discount | Lowering acquisition barrier |
| **Tiered** | More referrals = bigger rewards | Power referrers |
| **Milestone** | 3 referrals = free upgrade | Encouraging multiple referrals |

### Share Mechanisms
1. **In-product sharing** (highest conversion)
2. **Unique referral link**
3. **Email invite**
4. **Social sharing**
5. **Referral code**

## Affiliate Program Design

### Commission Structures

| Model | Example | Best For |
|-------|---------|----------|
| **% of sale** | 20-30% of first payment | Standard SaaS |
| **Recurring %** | 15-20% ongoing | Higher LTV products |
| **Flat fee** | $50 per conversion | Predictable costs |
| **Tiered** | 20% → 30% at volume | Motivating top performers |

### Affiliate Recruitment
- Existing customers (highest authenticity)
- Industry bloggers/reviewers
- YouTube creators in your space
- Newsletter operators
- Niche community leaders

## Key Metrics

| Metric | What It Measures | Good Benchmark |
|--------|-----------------|----------------|
| Active referrer rate | % of customers who refer | 5-15% |
| Referral conversion rate | Referred leads → customers | 10-25% |
| K-factor | Viral coefficient | >0.5 is good, >1 is viral |
| Referred customer LTV | Value vs. other channels | 16-25% higher |
| Referred customer churn | Retention vs. other channels | 18-37% lower |
| % of new customers from referrals | Channel contribution | 10-30% |

## Common Mistakes

- **Launching too early** — Before product-market fit
- **Weak incentives** — Reward doesn't motivate action
- **Hidden program** — Users don't know it exists
- **Bad timing** — Asking before value is experienced
- **Complex mechanics** — Too many rules or conditions
- **No tracking** — Can't measure what's working
- **Ignoring fraud** — Self-referrals, fake accounts
