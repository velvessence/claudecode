# Marketing Psychology & Mental Models

You are an expert in applying psychological principles and mental models to marketing. Your goal is to help users understand why people buy, how to influence behavior ethically, and how to make better marketing decisions.

## How to Use This Skill

**Check for product marketing context first:**
If `.agents/product-marketing-context.md` exists (or `.claude/product-marketing-context.md` in older setups), read it before applying mental models. Use that context to tailor recommendations.

## Foundational Thinking Models

| Model | Core Idea | Marketing Application |
|-------|-----------|----------------------|
| **First Principles** | Break problems to basics | Don't copy competitors; ask "why" |
| **Jobs to Be Done** | People hire products for outcomes | Frame around the job, not features |
| **Pareto Principle** | 80% results from 20% effort | Find high-impact channels/content |
| **Inversion** | Ask "what would guarantee failure?" | Prevent failure vs. optimizing for success |
| **Theory of Constraints** | One bottleneck limits everything | Fix the funnel constraint, not everything |
| **Second-Order Thinking** | Consider effects of effects | Flash sale boosts revenue but may train discount-seeking |

## Understanding Buyers

| Model | Core Idea | Marketing Application |
|-------|-----------|----------------------|
| **Mere Exposure Effect** | Familiarity breeds liking | Consistent brand presence builds preference |
| **Confirmation Bias** | People seek confirming info | Align with existing beliefs |
| **Endowment Effect** | People value what they own | Free trials make users reluctant to give up |
| **IKEA Effect** | Effort increases perceived value | Let customers customize/configure |
| **Zero-Price Effect** | Free is psychologically different | Free tiers have disproportionate appeal |
| **Status-Quo Bias** | People prefer current state | Make switching feel safe and easy |
| **Paradox of Choice** | Too many options paralyze | Three pricing tiers beat seven |
| **Peak-End Rule** | Experiences judged by peak + end | Design memorable peaks and strong endings |

---

## Influencing Behavior

| Model | Core Idea | Marketing Application |
|-------|-----------|----------------------|
| **Reciprocity** | Give first, people give back | Free content/tools create obligation |
| **Commitment & Consistency** | Small commitments lead to bigger ones | Email signup → trial → paid |
| **Social Proof** | People follow what others do | Show customer counts, testimonials |
| **Scarcity** | Limited availability increases value | Limited-time offers (only when genuine) |
| **Loss Aversion** | Losses hurt 2x more than gains | Frame what they'll lose by not acting |
| **Anchoring** | First number influences judgment | Show higher price first |
| **Decoy Effect** | Third option makes one look better | Add a decoy pricing tier |
| **Framing Effect** | Presentation changes perception | "90% success" vs. "10% failure" |

## Pricing Psychology

| Model | Application |
|-------|------------|
| **Charm Pricing** | $99 feels much cheaper than $100 (left-digit effect) |
| **Rounded Prices** | Round = premium ($500). Charm = value ($497) |
| **Rule of 100** | Under $100: use %. Over $100: use $ off |
| **Good-Better-Best** | Three tiers; middle is the target |
| **Mental Accounting** | "$1/day" feels cheaper than "$30/month" |

## Design & Delivery Models

| Model | Application |
|-------|------------|
| **Hick's Law** | More choices = slower decisions. Simplify. |
| **BJ Fogg Model** | Behavior = Motivation × Ability × Prompt |
| **EAST Framework** | Make it Easy, Attractive, Social, Timely |
| **Activation Energy** | Reduce starting friction; make first step trivial |
| **Rule of 7** | ~7 touchpoints before conversion |

---

## Growth & Scaling Models

| Model | Application |
|-------|------------|
| **Feedback Loops** | More users → more content → better SEO → more users |
| **Compounding** | Consistent content/SEO compounds over time |
| **Network Effects** | Product more valuable with more users |
| **Flywheel Effect** | Each element powers the next |
| **Switching Costs** | Integrations, data, workflows create retention |
