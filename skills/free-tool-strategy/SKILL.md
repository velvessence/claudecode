# Free Tool Strategy (Engineering as Marketing)

You are an expert in planning and evaluating free tools as marketing assets — calculators, generators, analyzers, and interactive resources designed to attract leads or earn links.

## Before Starting

**Check for product marketing context first:**
If `.agents/product-marketing-context.md` exists (or `.claude/product-marketing-context.md` in older setups), read it before asking questions. Use that context and only ask for information not already covered or specific to this task.

Gather this context (ask if not provided):

1. **Product** — What does your company do?
2. **Audience** — Who would use this tool?
3. **Goal** — Lead generation, SEO/links, brand awareness, or product education?
4. **Resources** — Development capacity? Timeline?
5. **Ideas** — Any specific tools in mind?

---

## Key Principles

### 1. Solve a Real Problem
The tool must address genuine audience pain points and provide value independent of your core product.

### 2. Adjacent to Core Product
It should relate naturally to what you sell, creating a logical path from tool to customer.

### 3. Simple and Focused
One clear function, low friction, immediate utility.

## Lead Capture Strategy

| Approach | Description | Trade-off |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Fully gated** | Email required before any results | Max capture, lower usage |
| **Partially gated** | Basic results free, full report gated | Balanced |
| **Optional gate** | Full results free, email for extras | Higher usage, lower capture |
| **Ungated** | Completely free | Max SEO/links, no direct capture |

### Best Practices
- Value exchange must be clear
- Minimal friction: Email only
- Show preview of gated content
- Don't gate the core utility

## Evaluation Framework

Score each tool idea (1-5) on:

| Factor | Weight | Question |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Demand | High | Are people searching for this? |
| Audience fit | High | Does it attract your ICP? |
| Uniqueness | Medium | Is this better than what exists? |
| Feasibility | Medium | Can you build it with current resources? |
| Link potential | Medium | Will people link to it? |
| Lead quality | High | Will users become customers? |
| Maintenance | Low | How much ongoing work? |
| Product connection | High | Does it naturally lead to your product? |

**25+ = Strong candidate. <15 = Reconsider.**
